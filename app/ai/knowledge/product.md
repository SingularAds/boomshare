## What Boomshare is

Boomshare is a desktop application for recording and sharing videos of your
screen, camera, or both. It is positioned as an alternative to Loom, with
additional functionality aimed at teams who share a lot of async video.

Typical use: instead of writing a long message or booking a meeting, you record
a short video walkthrough and send a link.

## Key features

- Screen recording (full screen, single window, or a selected region)
- Camera recording, including picture-in-picture over the screen
- System and microphone audio capture
- Instant share link generated as soon as the recording stops
- Automatic transcription and searchable captions
- Trim and cut edits without re-recording
- Viewer analytics: who watched, how much they watched
- Comments and reactions on a timestamp in the video
- Workspace/team libraries with folders and permissions
- Password-protected and expiring links

## Pricing

- **Free** — unlimited recordings up to 5 minutes each, 25 videos stored
- **Pro** — per user / month, unlimited recording length and storage, editing,
  analytics, password-protected links
- **Team** — per user / month, everything in Pro plus shared workspaces,
  admin controls and SSO

There is a free plan, so a customer can start without paying and without a
credit card.

## Installation

1. Download the installer for Windows or macOS.
2. Run the installer (takes about a minute).
3. Sign in or create an account.
4. Grant screen-recording permission when the OS asks.
5. Record the first video from the toolbar icon.

## Who it is for

- Support and customer success teams explaining fixes
- Sales teams sending personalised walkthroughs
- Engineers doing async code and bug walkthroughs
- Product and design teams giving feedback on work in progress
- Teachers and trainers recording lessons
- Anyone replacing a status meeting with a 3-minute video

## Benefits worth mentioning when relevant

- Faster than writing long explanations
- Removes meetings that only existed to demo something
- Recipients watch on their own schedule and at their own speed
- A recording is a reusable asset - record once, send many times
- Transcripts make videos searchable later

## Known limitations — state these honestly if asked

- No mobile recording app, and none announced
- No Linux build yet; it is in development, with no release date to share
- Live streaming is not supported; recordings only
- Offline recording works, but sharing requires an internet connection
